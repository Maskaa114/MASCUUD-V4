🔒 Locking rules

  • While a period is open, the evaluator can edit or delete a record.
  • When the period's end date passes (or it is closed early) all scores are locked.
  • A locked score can change only through a correction request, approved by the Vice Principal (Academic) and the Principal.
  • The original score is kept and every step is written to the audit trail below.
